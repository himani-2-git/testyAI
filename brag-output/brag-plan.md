# Brag Plan: testyAI

## What is this app?
testyAI is an AI-powered active-recall study platform that transforms lecture notes, multi-file PDFs, and slides into interactive 3D flashcards, auto-graded MCQ quizzes, and subject-level mastery analytics.

## The angle
"Passive re-reading is dead. Active recall is king."
testyAI isn't another generic AI summarizer—it actively forces your brain to retrieve knowledge through instant quizzes, flip cards, and targeted analytics that diagnose exactly what you need to review.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The punchy contrast: "Don't just read." followed by an electric highlighter strike revealing: "Get tested." Notes and multi-file PDFs drop in, immediately tagged with real-time subject classification (`ML`).

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **Interactive 3D Flashcard Flip:** A tactile notebook flashcard swings 180° in 3D space to reveal the exact formula and definition, accompanied by keyboard shortcuts.
2. **Instant MCQ Grading:** Selecting an option triggers instant verification—the correct answer flashes emerald green, showing "+100% Correct" and an AI-generated conceptual breakdown.
3. **Mastery Analytics & Streaks:** Subject performance metrics separate "Your Strengths (≥60%)" from "Areas to Improve (<75%)" alongside a 5-day streak milestone.

## Outro / punchline
The testyAI brand crest with tiger-orange flare: "Turn notes into mastery in seconds."

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry:** Uploading multi-file lecture notes / PDFs → Instant auto-tagging (`ML`).
2. **Key Action:** Active testing loop via 3D card flipping and instantaneous MCQ answer feedback.
3. **Result:** Dashboard performance diagnostics pinpointing weak spots (<75%) and study streak growth.
